\section{The estimator, and what it was checked against}
% Hayashi--Yoshida on the tau grid. Validated to zero error against known lags
% (EXP-010), including sign — EXP-010 caught a sign inversion that would have
% read as "Hyperliquid leads".
%
% Re-validated at Hyperliquid's own observation density (EXP-026): impose zero,
% thin the follower to HL's real print times, recover zero exactly.
% [TEST C] and again at the quote densities, which are 69x asymmetric.

\section{Result: a stable half-second lag}

\subsection{Magnitude and universality}
% 575 ms median, bootstrap CI [550, 575], mean 607. 100\% of 191 BTC hours and
% 100\% of 758 asset-hours, zero reversals. Per asset: BTC 575, ETH 550,
% HYPE 550, SOL 650.

\subsection{The control that makes it Hyperliquid's}
% OKX--Binance at 25 ms with the identical estimator and window — a factor of 23.
% The centralised venues do not lead one another (EXP-007).

\subsection{Volatility}
% No material dependence, but NOT literal invariance, and say which: pooled over
% 758 asset-hours holding trade frequency constant, rho = -0.100, p = 0.006.
% An effect explaining 1\% of variance does not move a 575 ms median; the word
% "invariant" is withdrawn. This is the sort of correction that buys credibility
% rather than costing it.

\section{What the lag is not}
% The core of the paper. Three pre-registered experiments, each with its
% falsifier written before the run.

\subsection{Not an artefact of unequal observation rates}
% EXP-026. Shift the leader by a KNOWN lag, thin to HL's real print times,
% require recovery. Exact in every hour on three of four assets, within 25 ms on
% the sparsest. The line that decides it is L = 0, where the estimator returns
% zero rather than drifting toward 575.

\subsection{Not the follower's own price innovations}
% EXP-027. Structural, not empirical: Hayashi--Yoshida is a cross-covariance, so
% an independent additive component contributes zero at every lag. Verified flat
% at noise ratios up to 80x the leader's variance.
%
% Report the honest limitation too: the synthetic follower does not reproduce a
% leader-thinning behaviour the real pair shows (P5). That manipulation degrades
% the LEADER, which the reported measurement never does — but say it, do not
% hide it.

\subsection{Not endogenous sampling}
% EXP-028. A total-variation clock samples on nothing but the series' own
% movement — a condition harsher than the venue's actual +0.06 to +0.17 coupling
% — and moves the peak by not one grid step at any asset or dimension.

\subsection{What that leaves}
% Block cadence, network latency, genuine price discovery. Observation density
% is out, and the cross-asset association (rho = -0.656) is attributed to market
% structure: sparser instruments really are slower.

\section{Two venues, two clocks}
% EXP-028 Part 1, and this is a finding in its own right rather than a robustness
% check. Spearman between prints-per-second and |return|:
%   Binance +0.28 to +0.77 ; Hyperliquid +0.06 to +0.17.
% Binance's trade arrival tracks price directly. Hyperliquid's barely does.
%
% The likely reason, measured: HL's quotes update ~7.9 times a second on BTC,
% about once per block. Trade arrival looks gated by block production rather than
% by price events. State it as consistent with two measurements, not as tested —
% an honest hedge that a referee will respect more than a confident mechanism.
